Perú

Perú is a country in south america. It is bordered by Brazil to the east, Colombia to the northeast, Ecuador to the north, Chile to the south, and a the pacific ocean to the west. It also touches a corner of Bolivia in the southeast. The country is a center of commerce and with a great variety of natural products and raw material to the development and production of a wide range of products.
